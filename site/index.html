About

Security-minded IT leader with 15+ years running technology and operations — currently IT Systems & Security Manager and E-commerce Operations lead at Belmont Leather Co., where I own identity, endpoint, network, and e-commerce platform security end to end. I administer the company's security stack daily: ESET EDR + ESET Cloud Protect across ~15 endpoints, UniFi networking with a 10-camera UniFi Protect surveillance system, and TrueNAS file storage/SMB infrastructure.

That infrastructure work has direct business results. I scaled the company's BigCommerce e-commerce platform and integrated it with our POS systems, driving 389% sales growth and $1.2M in new revenue while cutting $12K in costs through the POS/e-commerce integration. Building customer relationship systems added another $32K per year in revenue.

I graduated from Western Governors University in August 2026 with a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance. My capstone (D833) — a Wazuh SIEM deployment plus Duo MFA proposal for a fictional company, "Granite" — passed on first submission. My path there was non-linear, with prior coursework at SNHU, Rowan University, and NJIT.

What I do every day is incident response. I investigate phishing and business email compromise (BEC) attacks against the company, and I don't just block them — I take attacker infrastructure down through registrar, hosting provider, and abuse-desk reports. Recent real cases include:

  • A ClickFix social-engineering campaign targeting employees with fake fix-it prompts
  • A BEC attempt impersonating Beacon Adhesives to redirect payments
  • An SSA/ScreenConnect lure delivering remote-access malware
  • A Temu brand-impersonation phishing wave
  • A multi-message Punchbowl-themed phishing campaign

Outside of work you'll find me building CTF tooling, automating investigations with AI agents, and picking apart scam infrastructure for fun.
