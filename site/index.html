About

I run security and IT at Belmont Leather Co. — one person owning the whole stack: ESET EDR across ~15 endpoints, UniFi networking with 10-camera Protect surveillance, TrueNAS storage, identity, email security, and the BigCommerce e-commerce platform.

The part I actually love is incident response. When phishing or business email compromise hits the company, I investigate it, and I don't stop at blocking — I take attacker infrastructure down through registrar, hosting provider, and abuse-desk reports. Real cases I've worked:

  • A ClickFix social-engineering campaign targeting employees with fake fix-it prompts
  • A BEC attempt impersonating Beacon Adhesives to redirect payments
  • An SSA/ScreenConnect lure delivering remote-access malware
  • A Temu brand-impersonation phishing wave
  • A multi-message Punchbowl-themed phishing campaign

That hands-on work shows up in my projects too: Signal Vault (scam/phishing triage engine built from real cases), a CTF toolkit, and a Wazuh SIEM deployment for my capstone.

I backed all of this with the paper: B.S. Cybersecurity & Information Assurance from Western Governors University (August 2026, capstone passed first submission), plus Security+, CySA+, PenTest+, SSCP, and more — full list below.

On the business side, the platform work pays for itself: 389% sales growth, $1.2M in new revenue, $12K in costs cut through POS/e-commerce integration, and $32K/year added from customer relationship systems. Security that understands the business it protects.

Outside of work you'll find me building CTF tooling, automating investigations with AI agents, and picking apart scam infrastructure for fun.
