Fifteen credentials rendered as **3D challenge coins** on a curved wall — built with three.js and a few hundred lines of hand-rolled scene code. Drag to pan along the arc, tap any coin for its engraving (issuer, dates, credential ID) with a direct verification link. Includes a static accessible fallback for reduced-motion users and no-WebGL browsers; cert data is generated from the same markdown that powers this site.
