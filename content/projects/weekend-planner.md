A couples weekend-planning web app: real local events (never fabricated), swipe-to-match on plans with your partner, drive-time and weather awareness, taste tuning that learns from every vote, and AI-written event summaries. Built on Cloudflare Workers + D1 with an autonomous build→QA→ship pipeline and Sentry monitoring from day one.
