Local-first draft assistant for Sleeper fantasy football drafts. An Electron desktop app that tracks live picks in real time and ranks available players with a deterministic local engine (team needs, scarcity, superflex/2QB, PPR, injury overlap) — with optional LLM second opinions that stay advisory and can never override local rankings. Read-only by design; draft data never leaves your device.
