Free, open-source **Electron + React + TypeScript** desktop app for playing back Tesla USB drive footage — synchronized multi-camera dashcam/Sentry playback kept under ~2 frames of drift, a live telemetry HUD (speed, G-forces, throttle, lap data) for Track Mode sessions, and a synced GPS map drawing the full ~45 Hz trace. Everything runs locally; footage never leaves your machine.
